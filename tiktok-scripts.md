## Filming Notes
- Keep it conversational — like talking to a friend, not presenting
- Eye contact with camera lens the whole time
- Natural gestures, don't be stiff
- Film in a clean but not overly staged setting
- Good lighting on face, minimal background noise
- Can use subtle zoom-ins on key moments for emphasis
- End cards: "Link in bio" can be spoken + text overlay
