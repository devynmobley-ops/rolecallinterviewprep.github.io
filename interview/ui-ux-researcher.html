Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Walk me through your research process when starting a new product design project.
Tip: Outline a clear methodology: define objectives, identify user segments, choose methods (interviews, surveys, usability tests), recruit participants, synthesize findings, and present actionable insights.
Q2
How do you decide between qualitative and quantitative research methods for a given project?
Tip: Explain that qualitative methods (interviews, diary studies) uncover the 'why' behind behaviors, while quantitative methods (surveys, analytics, A/B tests) measure the 'what' and 'how many.' Match method to research questions and project stage.
Q3
Tell me about a time your research findings contradicted stakeholder assumptions. How did you handle it?
Tip: Show diplomatic communication skills. Describe presenting data objectively, using direct user quotes or video clips, and framing findings as opportunities rather than criticisms.
Q4
What tools do you use for conducting and analyzing user research?
Tip: Mention specific tools like UserTesting, Maze, Dovetail, Optimal Workshop, Lookback, or similar. Explain how you use them for different research phases and how you organize and share findings.
