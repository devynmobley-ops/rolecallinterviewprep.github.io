Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Describe your training and any hands-on experience with overhead or underground line work.
Tip: Be specific about climbing school, apprenticeship, military, or contractor experience — pole climbing, framing, terminations — and honest about the limits of your scope.
Q2
How do you approach safety on a line crew, and what does a good job briefing look like?
Tip: Cover tailboard briefings, hazard identification, PPE (FR clothing, hard hat, rubber goods), grounds, minimum approach distances, and clear role assignments.
Q3
Tell me about a time you worked in bad weather or during an emergency outage.
Tip: Storm response realities, long hours, prioritizing critical loads like hospitals, working safely while fatigued, and crew rest discipline.
Q4
Why do you want to be a lineworker, and are you ready for heights, climbing, and hard physical work?
Tip: Give genuine motivation, evidence of physical readiness (climbing school, fitness prep), and commitment to the on-call lifestyle.

Education & Certifications

Degrees

  • No degree required
  • AS Electrical Technology
  • Apprenticeship certificate

Certifications

  • CDL Class A license (state DMV — required to drive line trucks)
  • NCCER Power Line Worker certification
  • OSHA 10-Hour Construction Outreach (DOL)

Career Paths

  • IBEW/NJATC outside line apprenticeship (3-4 years, earn while you learn)
  • Community college lineworker certificate program (10-15 weeks) followed by a utility or contractor apprenticeship
  • Military electrical or power generation training (for example Army Prime Power or Navy Seabees) converted to a utility hire
