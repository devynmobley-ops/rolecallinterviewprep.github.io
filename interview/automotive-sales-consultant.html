Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Walk me through how you greet a new customer on the lot and qualify what they are looking for.
Tip: Cover the meet-and-greet, building quick rapport, and discovery questions on budget, trade-in, vehicle use, and timeline. Emphasize a needs-based, low-pressure approach.
Q2
Describe a time you turned a hesitant browser into a buyer.
Tip: Use STAR with a specific objection you uncovered and resolved, the follow-up you did, and a quantified outcome (units, gross profit, or CSI score).
Q3
How do you handle a customer who says they need to think about it or is shopping other dealers?
Tip: Acknowledge the concern, dig for the real objection (price, trade value, payment), give a concrete reason to return such as a scheduled test drive or hold, and log the follow-up in the CRM.
Q4
What is your approach to hitting a monthly sales target?
Tip: Talk daily activity discipline, pipeline and follow-up cadence, and how you track units and gross. Pitfall: vague motivation talk with no numbers.

Education & Certifications

Degrees

  • BS Business Administration
  • BS Marketing
  • No degree required

Certifications

  • NADA/ATD Dealer Academy sales certification
  • State motor vehicle salesperson license (where required)
  • CDK Global or Reynolds & Reynolds DMS certification

Career Paths

  • Start as a lot attendant or sales BDC rep at a dealership and promote into sales
  • Community college or NADA/ATD sales courses plus on-the-job training
  • Retail or customer-facing sales experience plus manufacturer brand certification training
