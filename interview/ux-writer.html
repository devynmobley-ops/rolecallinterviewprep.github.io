Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Walk me through how you approach writing copy for a user flow you've never worked with before.
Tip: Show a research-first process: use the product yourself, talk to support/PMs for real user language, review analytics and support tickets, then draft, test, and iterate.
Q2
Tell me about a time your writing measurably improved a user experience.
Tip: Give a STAR story with numbers: before/after conversion, task completion, error rates, or support ticket volume — even small percentages show you measure impact.
Q3
How do you balance brand voice with clarity and brevity in UI text?
Tip: Prioritize clarity over cleverness in high-stakes moments (errors, payments), and explain how a voice-and-tone guide encodes when to be playful vs. plain.
Q4
How do you handle disagreement with a designer or PM about the words in a design?
Tip: Show partnership, not ownership battles: invite them into the problem, bring user evidence or test results, and be willing to A/B test rather than argue taste.
