Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Walk me through a typical cardiac catheterization procedure and your role in it.
Tip: Cover patient prep, sterile field setup, hemodynamic monitoring, equipment checks, recording pressures and waveforms, patient monitoring, and documentation. Show you know the whole workflow, not one slice.
Q2
How do you set up and verify hemodynamic monitoring equipment before a case?
Tip: Zero and calibrate transducers, level to the phlebostatic axis, verify waveform quality, confirm patient data, and check alarms. Mention the double-check habits that prevent wrong readings.
Q3
A patient's vital signs change unexpectedly during a procedure. What do you do?
Tip: Report immediately to the physician, keep monitoring and documenting, stay calm, and act within your scope — assist, do not independently treat. Mention ACLS awareness and closed-loop communication.
Q4
How do you maintain radiation safety in the cath lab?
Tip: Lead aprons and thyroid shields, dosimeter discipline, maximizing distance, minimizing fluoro time, collimation, and pregnancy checks. Reference ALARA and monitoring records.

Education & Certifications

Degrees

  • BS Health Sciences
  • BS Biomedical Sciences
  • AS Radiologic Technology

Certifications

  • CCI Registered Cardiovascular Invasive Specialist (RCIS)
  • Basic Life Support (BLS) and Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS)
  • CCI Certified Rhythm Analyst Technician (CRAT)

Career Paths

  • CAAHEP-accredited associate degree in cardiovascular technology (2 years) with clinical rotations
  • Hospital-based cardiovascular technology certificate program with hands-on cath lab training
  • Cross-train from EMT/paramedic, nursing, or monitoring tech roles into the cath lab while pursuing RCIS
