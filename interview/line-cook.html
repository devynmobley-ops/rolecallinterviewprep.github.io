Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Describe your experience working a station during a dinner rush. How do you stay organized?
Tip: Show command of the line: mise en place before service, ticket prioritization (first-in-first-out, fire times), communication with expo and other stations, and calm pacing under pressure.
Q2
How do you handle food safety and sanitation on your station?
Tip: Name specifics: temperature logs and danger-zone rules, handwashing and glove changes, separate boards for raw proteins, sanitizer bucket ratios, date-labeling and FIFO rotation.
Q3
A modified ticket comes in during peak service. Walk me through how you handle it.
Tip: Show accuracy and communication: read the ticket back, flag the mod verbally to the station and expo, isolate allergen-free plating, and never guess at an unclear modification.
Q4
Tell me about a time you made a mistake during service. What did you do?
Tip: Own it fast: immediate correction and honesty with the chef/expo, no hiding remakes, and a concrete prevention step (better labeling, slower double-check, mise adjustment).
