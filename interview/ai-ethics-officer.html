Sample Interview Questions

Q1
How do you detect and measure bias in AI systems?
Tip: Discuss statistical fairness metrics (demographic parity, equalized odds), disparate impact analysis, testing across protected groups, and using bias auditing tools throughout the ML lifecycle.
Q2
How would you establish a responsible AI policy for an organization?
Tip: Cover stakeholder engagement, defining ethical principles, creating review processes, establishing governance boards, training programs, and aligning with industry frameworks like NIST AI RMF.
Q3
Describe your approach to algorithmic auditing.
Tip: Explain the audit lifecycle: scoping, data review, model testing, outcome analysis, documentation, and recommendations. Include both technical testing and process/policy review.
Q4
How do you communicate AI ethics concerns to technical and business stakeholders?
Tip: Discuss tailoring the message to the audience, using concrete examples and case studies, quantifying risk, proposing actionable mitigations, and building cross-functional alignment.
