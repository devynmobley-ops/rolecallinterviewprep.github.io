Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Describe your experience touring apartments with prospective residents and closing the lease.
Tip: Cover discovery (move-in date, budget, must-haves), matching the unit and amenities to their needs, creating urgency, and asking for the application.
Q2
A prospect says your rent is too high compared with the complex down the street. How do you respond?
Tip: Frame value (location, utilities, amenities, condition, incentives), avoid badmouthing competitors, and use only the concessions you are authorized to offer.
Q3
Tell me about a time you turned an unhappy resident into a renewal.
Tip: Show empathy, fast follow-through with maintenance or management, and quantify retention or renewal results where you can.
Q4
How do you stay organized while juggling multiple prospects, tours, and follow-ups?
Tip: Name the property management and CRM tools you use (Yardi, RealPage, Entrata, Knock), a follow-up cadence, and speed-to-lead discipline.

Certifications

  • Certified Apartment Leasing Professional (CALP) — National Apartment Association
  • Fair Housing certification (NAA Fair Housing on Demand or equivalent)
  • Real estate salesperson license (where the role requires it)

Career Paths

  • NAA CALP coursework (four courses plus exam) while working as a leasing assistant
  • On-the-job training at a property management company with a mentor
  • Community college property management certificate plus a leasing internship
