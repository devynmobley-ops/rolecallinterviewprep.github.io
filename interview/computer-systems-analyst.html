Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Tell me about a time you analyzed a business process and recommended a system change.
Tip: Use STAR. Show how you gathered user requirements, modeled the as-is and to-be process, evaluated options, and measured the result (time saved, errors reduced, cost avoided).
Q2
How do you gather and document requirements from non-technical stakeholders?
Tip: Interviews, workshops, observation, user stories and use cases — then confirm with walkthroughs and sign-off. Stress translating business language into clear functional specs.
Q3
How do you decide between building a solution and buying or configuring off-the-shelf software?
Tip: Use a fit-gap and total-cost-of-ownership lens: implementation effort, integration costs, maintenance burden, time to value, and vendor risk. Show a decision process, not a preference.
Q4
Walk me through how you would test a new system before rollout.
Tip: Test cases traced to requirements, UAT with real users, regression testing, a pilot group, acceptance criteria, sign-off, and a rollback plan. Name your role in each step.
