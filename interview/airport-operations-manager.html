Sample Interview Questions

Q1
How do you coordinate daily ramp, terminal, and airside operations to ensure on-time performance?
Tip: Discuss integrated ops centers, real-time dashboards, and cross-functional briefings with airlines and ground handlers.
Q2
Describe your experience managing irregular operations such as weather delays or diversions.
Tip: Walk through your IROPS playbook: passenger rebooking coordination, gate management, and communication protocols.
Q3
How do you ensure compliance with FAA Part 139 airport certification requirements?
Tip: Reference self-inspection programs, wildlife management, pavement conditions, and ARFF readiness standards.
Q4
What is your approach to managing airside safety and reducing runway incursions?
Tip: Talk about driver training programs, movement area hot-spot analysis, and FOD prevention campaigns.
