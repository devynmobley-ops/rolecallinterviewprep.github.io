Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Describe the interpreting modes you work in and when each is appropriate.
Tip: Cover all three: consecutive (witness testimony, depositions), simultaneous (hearings, judge's instructions), and sight translation (documents) — and note mode-switching discipline in live proceedings.
Q2
You hear a term with no direct equivalent in the target language. How do you handle it?
Tip: Show technique: approximate with the closest legal/cultural equivalent, briefly note the gap for the record if needed, and prepare bilingual glossaries in advance so this is rare.
Q3
What do you do if you realize you made an interpretation error mid-proceeding?
Tip: Correct immediately and on the record — accuracy outweighs embarrassment. Say you'll pause, state the correction clearly, and never let an error ride to protect appearances.
Q4
How do you maintain impartiality and confidentiality in proceedings?
Tip: Know the code of ethics (NAJIT/IMIA): first-person interpretation, no additions or omissions, no side conversations with parties, and strict confidentiality even about cases you're done with.
