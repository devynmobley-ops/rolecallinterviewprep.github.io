Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Describe your process for conducting a functional behavior assessment.
Tip: Cover indirect methods (interviews, ABC data), descriptive assessment, functional analysis when appropriate, and a precise hypothesis statement naming setting, antecedents, behavior, and consequence.
Q2
Tell me about a behavior intervention plan you designed and how you measured whether it worked.
Tip: Highlight function-matched strategies across antecedent, teaching, and consequence layers, data-based decision rules, implementation fidelity checks, and outcomes.
Q3
How do you train and supervise RBTs or classroom staff to implement plans with fidelity?
Tip: Reference behavioral skills training (instruction, modeling, rehearsal, feedback), competency checks, ongoing observation, and interobserver agreement.
Q4
What data systems do you use, and how do you make treatment decisions from data?
Tip: Name measurement types (frequency, duration, latency), graphing and visual analysis, single-case design, and platforms like CentralReach or Catalyst.

Education & Certifications

Degrees

  • BS Psychology
  • MS Counseling Psychology
  • BS Special Education

Certifications

  • Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) — Behavior Analyst Certification Board (BACB)
  • Registered Behavior Technician (RBT) — BACB
  • Board Certified Assistant Behavior Analyst (BCaBA) — BACB

Career Paths

  • Master's degree plus BACB-approved coursework and supervised fieldwork hours
  • Start as an RBT with a bachelor's, then complete a BACB-verified master's program
  • Education or psychology degree plus an ABA graduate certificate and supervised experience
