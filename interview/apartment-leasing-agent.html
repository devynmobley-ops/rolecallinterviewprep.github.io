Sample Interview Questions

Q1
Walk me through how you'd give a tour to a prospective renter.
Tip: Show a consultative flow: discovery questions first (move timeline, must-haves, budget), tour the model and amenities tailored to their answers, highlight lifestyle benefits, and close with clear next steps on applying.
Q2
How do you handle objections like price, location, or missing amenities?
Tip: Acknowledge-clarify-respond: ask what's behind the objection, reframe value (utilities, amenities, commute, specials), and know when to let a prospect go rather than over-push.
Q3
How do you follow up with leads who haven't signed a lease?
Tip: Show organized persistence: a follow-up cadence across phone, email, and text, personalized notes referencing their needs, urgency created by real availability, and CRM notes after every touch.
Q4
What leasing or property management software have you used?
Tip: Name tools concretely (Yardi, RealPage, AppFolio, Knock, RentCafe) and how you used them for leads, guest cards, applications, and reporting — software familiarity is a common hiring filter.

Education & Certifications

Degrees

  • BS Real Estate
  • BS Business Administration
  • High school diploma

Certifications

  • Certified Apartment Leasing Professional (CALP) - NAA
  • Certified Apartment Manager (CAM) - NAA
  • Real estate license (state, where required)

Career Paths

  • High school diploma + on-the-job training at a property management company
  • NAA CALP course through a local apartment association (6 months)
  • Retail or sales experience + leasing certification for a career switch into property management
